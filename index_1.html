Meet the artist

Assalamu alaikum, I'm Saira

Islamic Essence is where I share the things I love most: duas from the Qur'an and Sunnah, the beauty of Arabic calligraphy, and Islamic art for your home.

I letter every piece by hand, stroke by stroke, with an Apple Pencil on iPad. Each one reaches you as a crisp, high-resolution file you can print in any size. My ebooks help you learn and live your deen, starting with a dua book that has audio for every dua. My art and custom names are made to bring barakah and beauty to your walls.

May Allah bless you and your family. Saira
