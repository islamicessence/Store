Questions

Before you buy

I can't read Arabic. Is this for me?

Yes. Every dua has an easy transliteration and an English meaning, and the audio lets you hear exactly how it is said.

Are the duas authentic?

All duas are from the Qur'an and the Sunnah, and each one shows its source so you can check it.

How do I get the book?

Right after payment you get a download link for the PDF. It opens on any phone, tablet or computer.

How does the audio work?

Tap the Listen button under a dua, or scan its QR code with your phone camera. The recitation opens in your browser, so you need an internet connection to play it.

Can I print it?

Yes, at home or at a print shop. The QR codes still work on paper.
