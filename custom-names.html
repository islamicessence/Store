Questions

Good to know

My name isn't Arabic. Can you still write it?

Yes. Names from any language are written by how they sound, so names like Sarah, Daniel or Maria all work.

Can I have two names together?

Yes. Couples and siblings are popular. Mention both names when you send your details.

Can I choose the colours?

Yes. Tell Saira your favourite colours, or the room it will hang in, and she will design around them.

Is it handwritten or digital?

Each piece is lettered by hand, stroke by stroke, with an Apple Pencil on iPad. You receive crisp, high-resolution digital files.

Do you ship a printed piece?

No. You receive digital files ready to print at any photo lab or at home, in every common frame size.
